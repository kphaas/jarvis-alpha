## 3. Messaging pillars

Three claims everything should ladder up to:

1. **Private by design.** Runs on your hardware. Nothing sold, shared, or sent to the cloud.
2. **Memory that never resets.** Continuity across every conversation and every domain.
3. **Autonomy you control.** It acts — but only inside guardrails you set and can see.

**Brand value (underneath all three):** *Built for how your mind works.* Neurodiversity-first — structure over walls of text, choices over paragraphs, clarity as a feature.

## 4. Voice & tone

**Attributes:** Calm. Precise. Human. Confident, never hype. Quietly futuristic.

**We sound like** a trusted, technical friend — grounded and clear, never a sales pitch.

**Do**
- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Concrete claims ("memory that never resets"), not adjectives ("powerful, revolutionary").
- Lead with the benefit, then the mechanism.
- Respect the reader's intelligence.

**Don't**
- Buzzwords: "revolutionary," "game-changing," "next-gen," "AI-powered."
- Fear or hype. No exclamation spam.
- Overpromise. If it's planned, say "planned."

**Before → After**
- ❌ "Revolutionary AI-powered platform that transforms your entire life!"
- ✅ "A private AI that runs your life — and remembers everything."

## 5. Naming rules

**The product**
- **Written:** `AT0` — capital A, capital T, numeral zero. Never `AT-0`, `ATO`, `At0`, `at0`.
- **Spoken:** "Auto."
- **First mention pattern:** `AT0 ("Auto")`, then `AT0` after.
- The `0` is the beacon ring — it *is* the logo.

**Sub-products**
- Evocative one-word, Title Case for tools: **Forge** (build code), **Smithy** (ideas → specs), **Crucible** (3D print / fabricate), **Spark** (persona / voice).
- Plain descriptive for life domains: **Family**, **Financial**, **Medical**.
- The whole suite is pronounced "Auto."
