## LinkedIn — launch post

I'm dyslexic. For most of my life, *how computers expect you to work* and *how my brain actually works* were two different things.

So I built my own AI to close that gap. It's called **AT0** (say "Auto") — a private AI that runs my whole life, and remembers everything.

Cloud assistants share three problems: they forget you every session, they run on someone else's servers, and your life becomes their data. Fine for trivia. Not for your life.

AT0 is built differently:

🔒 **Private by design** — it runs on my own hardware. Nothing sold, shared, or sent to the cloud.
🧠 **Memory that never resets** — continuity across every conversation and every domain.
🎛️ **Autonomy I control** — it acts on my behalf, but only inside guardrails I can see.

The part most tools miss: good AI isn't just the model — it's the interface to your *thinking*. As a dyslexic builder, I work best with structure over walls of text and choices over paragraphs. Once I shaped AI around how my mind actually works, prompting stopped being a workaround and became an unlock. AT0 is built on that: **AI should meet your mind where it already is.**

It's not a chatbot — it's a system. One beacon, with modules for the real domains of life: Family, Financial, Medical, a code pipeline (Forge + Smithy), fabrication (Crucible), and a persona (Spark).

I'm building it in public. If you care about private AI, self-hosting, or tools built for neurodivergent minds, I'd love for you to follow along.

Early access is opening in waves — request yours at **at-0.com**.

Your whole life, on Auto.
