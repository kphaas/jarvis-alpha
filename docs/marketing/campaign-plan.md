# AT0 — Campaign Plan (X + LinkedIn)

**Goal:** Build an audience + an early-access waitlist by telling the AT0 story, building in public.
**Frame:** Both — my story *and* a waitlist.
**Channels:** X (Twitter) + LinkedIn.
**Signups go to:** the at-0.com request form → jarvisaihaas@gmail.com.

---

## 1. Who I'm talking to

- Self-hosting / privacy people (own their data, run their own stack)
- AI builders & tinkerers (local models, agents)
- Neurodivergent folks who feel underserved by mainstream tools
- "Quietly technical" professionals who want AI for their *life*, not just work

## 2. The angle (what makes people stop scrolling)

Three story hooks, rotate them:
1. **Neurodiversity** — "Built for how my mind works." (your differentiator — lead here)
2. **Privacy / ownership** — "Cloud AI forgets you and owns your data. Mine doesn't."
3. **The system** — "Not a chatbot. One beacon, my whole life."

Everything ladders back to the 3 pillars: **Private · Memory that never resets · Autonomy you control.**

## 3. Cadence (sustainable for a solo builder)

| Channel | Rhythm | Format |
|---|---|---|
| X | 3–5×/week | build-in-public notes, screenshots, 1 thread/week |
| LinkedIn | 1–2×/week | longer story posts, the article, milestones |

Rule: **show, don't announce.** Real screenshots > promises. Say "planned" when it's planned.

## 4. 4-week launch calendar

**Week 0 — Set the stage**
- Update profiles: avatar-at0, banners, bios (from the Messaging Kit)
- Publish the **Why-AT0 article** (LinkedIn article + /why page)
- Pin the launch thread on X

**Week 1 — Launch**
- Day 1: X launch thread + LinkedIn launch post
- Day 2–3: System-map graphic ("one beacon, your whole life")
- Day 4–5: Pillar cards (Private / Memory / Autonomy), one per post

**Week 2 — Proof & depth**
- Comparison graphic (AT0 vs cloud assistants)
- A build-in-public post: one real thing you shipped (with screenshot)
- The neurodiversity card + a short personal post

**Week 3 — Momentum**
- Carousel (LinkedIn): the full story in 7 slides
- Animated teaser (X/LinkedIn header + a post)
- Behind-the-scenes: an architecture decision, told simply

**Week 4 — Convert**
- "Early access is opening" post → drive to at-0.com waitlist
- Recap thread of what you've shared + what's next
- One-pager PDF for anyone who asks "what is this?"

## 6. Conversion path

Every piece ends with **one** ask: *Request early access → at-0.com.*
The site captures the email (Web3Forms). Reply to each signup personally — early users are your best amplifiers.

## 7. What to measure (keep it simple)

- Waitlist signups (the one that matters)
- Profile visits + follower growth (X, LinkedIn)
- Which **angle** drives the most signups (neurodiversity vs privacy vs system) → do more of that

## 8. Guardrails (your brand voice)

- Calm, concrete, no hype. No "revolutionary."
- Don't overpromise — "planned" means planned.
- Lead with the benefit, then the mechanism.
- Structure over walls of text (practice what AT0 preaches).
