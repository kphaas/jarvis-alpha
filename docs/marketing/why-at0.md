# Built for how my mind works: why I'm building AT0

> AT0 ("Auto") is a private, self-hosted AI that runs my whole life — and remembers everything.

I'm dyslexic. For most of my life, *how computers expect you to work* and *how my brain actually works* were two different things. AT0 is me closing that gap — and building the AI I always wanted in the process.

## The problem with cloud AI

Every cloud assistant has the same three problems:

1. **It forgets you.** Every session starts from zero. No continuity, no context, no memory of what mattered yesterday.
2. **It isn't yours.** It runs on someone else's servers, on their terms.
3. **You're the product.** Your life becomes their training data.

For anyone that's fine for trivia and email drafts. But I didn't want an assistant for *tasks*. I wanted one for my *life*.

## Built for how my mind works

Here's the part most tools miss: good AI isn't just about the model. It's about the *interface to your thinking*.

As a dyslexic builder, I work best with **structure over walls of text, choices over paragraphs, and facts kept separate from assumptions and risks.** Once I started shaping AI around how my mind actually works, prompting stopped being a workaround. It became an **unlock**.

AT0 is built on that idea: **AI should meet your mind where it already is** — not the other way around. Clarity isn't a nice-to-have. It's a feature.

## What makes AT0 different

Three things, and they're the whole point:

- **Private by design.** It runs on my own hardware. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to the cloud.
- **Memory that never resets.** Continuity across every conversation and every domain. It remembers.
- **Autonomy you control.** It acts on my behalf — but only inside guardrails I set and can see.

## One beacon, your whole life

AT0 isn't a chatbot. It's a system. One beacon at the center, with modules for the real domains of life:

- **Family** · **Financial** · **Medical** — the life domains
- **Forge** (builds code) · **Smithy** (turns ideas into specs) · **Crucible** (3D printing / fabrication) — the maker tools
- **Spark** — the persona

All on my own hardware. All remembering. All under guardrails I can see.
