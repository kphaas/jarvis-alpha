# Herald Press Outreach Runbook

Updated: 2026-06-28

## Scope

Herald press outreach is draft-only support for AT0 press work:

- Maintain a sourced press target list.
- Draft up to 5 personalized pitches per week.
- Keep every pitch human-reviewed before any send.
- Never scrape private emails or infer contact data not shown on a public source.
- Never auto-send, auto-follow up, or publish on behalf of AT0.

This is the P5 Herald outreach lane from `docs/specs/jarvis-herald-spec.md`:
suggest and draft only.

## Public Proof

Use this public proof path in pitch drafts:

- Press kit: `https://at-0.com/press/`
- Public contact: `hello@at-0.com`
- Security reports: `security@at-0.com`
- Privacy requests: `privacy@at-0.com`

Approved proof points:

- Public press kit and approved positioning are live at `at-0.com/press`.
- Sanitized capability cards show Alpha, Helm, Family, Finance, Forge, and Print boundaries.
- High-stakes workflows remain human-approved and draft-first.
- AT0 is in controlled early-access waves, not broad public availability.
- AT0 must not claim customer metrics, partnerships, compliance certifications, or autonomous high-stakes action unless separately verified.

## Weekly Operating Loop

| Step | Owner | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Revalidate | Herald + Ken | Reopen the public source link before drafting. If the beat moved, skip. |
| 2. Pick 5 | Herald | Use `MAX_WEEKLY_PRESS_DRAFTS = 5`; never batch more without explicit approval. |
| 3. Draft | Herald | Personalize to public beat, outlet, and story angle only. |
| 4. Review | Ken | Edit facts, screenshots, proof points, and timing. |
| 5. Send | Ken | Manual send only from the approved mailbox until a press-send route is designed and reviewed. |
| 6. Record | Herald | Track sent date, response, follow-up decision, and source URL. |

## Fact Boundaries

Never say:

- AT0 is broadly available.
- AT0 has customer counts, revenue, funding, partnerships, certifications, or press coverage unless verified.
- AT0 gives medical, financial, or legal instructions autonomously.
- AT0 will auto-send emails, social posts, or outreach without approval.

Safe phrasing:

- "AT0 is being opened in controlled early-access waves."
- "AT0 is private AI infrastructure from Ken Haas."
- "The current proof kit uses sanitized cards and walkthroughs to avoid exposing private data."
- "High-stakes workflows remain human-approved and draft-first."
